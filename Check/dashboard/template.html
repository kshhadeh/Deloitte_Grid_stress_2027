Method and limitations

  • Demand = Liander standard yearly consumption (SJV) for small connections; Liander changed its definition in 2025, so 2024→2025 is not a growth pair.
  • Projection: measured transformer peak × buurt growth factor; two years ahead because the peaks are from 2025.
  • Peaks mix consumption and solar feed-in. Buurt utilisation follows consumption per kW (Spearman 0.60) much more than feed-in per kW (0.27), so consumption growth is the central case.
  • Feed-in grew from 2.74 to 3.27 TWh (+19%); about +7% per year after removing the sunnier 2025.
  • 104 buurten have transformers but no small connections (business or recreation areas); they use national growth only.
  • No local EV data; the stress label partly reflects buurt size; CBS privacy hides some values in small buurten.
